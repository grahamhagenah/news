# Options at the end of a line: limit=5 (at most 5 posts), days=14 (keep posts for 14 days), and
# only="Full Performance" (only posts whose titles have that in them, in any case), tag="BNM" (a label beside
# each of its posts' titles) and titles=page (each post's title from its own page).
# Defaults are 15 posts from the last 3 days.
# A podcast's episodes link to Apple Podcasts, the show found by searching its name and told apart by the
# feed it carries. Where a show publishes under a feed address Apple doesn't list, apple=<show id> names
# it outright (the id is the number after /id in its podcasts.apple.com address).
